☾ about me

hey, i'm jess. night owl, collector of mixtapes, professional overthinker. i made jesscel.com because i missed when the internet felt like somebody's bedroom wall: messy, personal, and covered in things they loved.

i write bad poetry, make playlists for moods that don't have names, and take too many blurry photos at shows. if you're here, you're probably a little like me.
